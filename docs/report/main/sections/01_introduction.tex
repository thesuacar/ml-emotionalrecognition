\section{Introduction and research questions}
This report studies facial expression recognition with classical machine learning on two datasets, FER2013 and KDEF, and asks which challenges are specific to each dataset and how well knowledge transfers between them. The FER2013 baseline classifiers are covered in Task 1. Tasks 2 to 6 cover the KDEF study, cross-dataset generalization, the fuzzy classifier, the creative challenge and the real-time demo. 

The primary question is how datasets and feature/model choices affect within-dataset accuracy and cross-dataset generalization. Secondary questions concern which emotions and representations are most transferable, whether interpretable facial geometry is useful, and how reliable a trained model remains under webcam conditions.
